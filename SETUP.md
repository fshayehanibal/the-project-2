## How to Play
- Open `index.html` in a web browser
- Click or tap the START button to begin
- Use arrow keys to move your ship
- Press SPACE to fire bullets
- Catch fish and defeat pirates to progress through levels
- Boss levels require multiple hits to defeat

## Game Levels
- **Level 1**: Catch 20 fish OR defeat 20 pirates
- **Level 2**: Repeat the same (fish can now attack)
- **Level 3 & 4**: Boss fights (pirates and giant fish)
- Each enemy killed/caught resets per-level counters
- Player health: 20

## Controls
- Arrow Keys: Move
- SPACE: Fire bullets
- Radar shows nearby fishes and pirates
